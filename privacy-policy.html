Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how One-Tap Rush (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the game on Android. The App is designed as an offline-first arcade experience. We do not require accounts, and we do not operate a game backend or cloud save service.

Short summary
  • No login or user account is required.
  • Game progress (scores, coins, settings, unlocks) is stored only on your device.
  • Optional advertising is provided by Google AdMob, which may collect and process data under Google’s policies.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information we store on your device

The App stores gameplay and preference data locally using an on-device database (SQLite). This data typically includes:

  • Best score and completed run history
  • Coin balance and skin unlocks
  • Settings such as sound effects, music preference, and haptics
  • Simple achievement / daily-reward flags (to avoid duplicate rewards)

This information is not uploaded to our servers because the App does not use a developer-operated cloud database, account system, or online leaderboard.

If you uninstall the App or clear the App’s storage in Android settings, this local data may be permanently deleted.

3. Information we do not collect directly

We do not intentionally collect or request:

  • Name, email address, or password (no registration)
  • Precise GPS location for gameplay
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data
  • Payment card details inside the App (the App has no in-app subscription system operated by us)

Ads are served by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google may collect and process device and advertising-related information in accordance with Google’s own policies, which may include identifiers such as the advertising ID, IP address, device information, and interaction data used for ad serving, fraud prevention, and measurement.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA/UK), consent or other legal bases for personalized ads may be requested via Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) or equivalent consent flows.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to Google in connection with ads, please review Google’s privacy tools and contact Google as appropriate.
